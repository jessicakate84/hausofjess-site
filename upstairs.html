Haus of Jess · Life outside work

Upstairs at the Haus

A look at life outside the boardroom: travel, music, art, and now and then the quiet part said out loud.

Downstairs is business. Up here is everything else: the places I love, the things that keep me steady, and what it's actually like to walk into the room as me.

Home base

Deep Ellum

Home is Deep Ellum, Dallas’s original music district, and it suits me. Strong coffee by morning, crates of vinyl by afternoon, live music loud enough to rattle your ribs by night. It’s loud, it’s creative, it’s unapologetically itself, and its history runs deeper than most cities’. Murals on every wall, a band on every corner, and not once has it asked permission to be fabulous. Honestly? Same.

Sound

Every genre, on repeat

Gospel raised my standards: Fred Hammond, Kirk Franklin, and Hezekiah Walker and the Love Fellowship Crusade Choir, still undefeated. Since then it’s been country, my friend Jenni’s punk band Tomgirls, New York house, Taylor, and Olivia Dean on a loop I refuse to apologize for. At fourteen I agonized over East Coast or West Coast. A good bass line still gets me dancing.

Travel

Comfort, not status

Main cabin or up front, I’m flexible. But I’m tall, darling, so legroom isn’t a luxury. It’s survival. Perfect travel: laptop closed, earbuds in, great music on. Overnight? Face mask, under-eye patches and a cheeky glass of wine. Arrive rested, and look like it. And when I get to choose where I sleep, it’s a Ritz-Carlton. Every time.

Art

Art & adventures

Wherever I land, I go looking for art, the kind that stops you mid-step. In London a few weeks ago, Paul Delaroche’s The Execution of Lady Jane Grey did exactly that at the National Gallery. It spoke to me in a way I’m still unpacking, and every trip is better with a gallery stop. There’s a whole piece coming on why that painting got me.
